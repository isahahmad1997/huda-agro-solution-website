About Huda Agro-solution Limited

A farm business should be built with purpose.

Huda Agro-solution Limited focuses on livestock farming and agribusiness consultation in Nigeria. Our livestock focus includes cattle, goats and sheep, poultry, fish farming, and rabbit farming.

Our portfolio brings together production planning, day-to-day farm management, feeding considerations, farm records, biosecurity awareness, and business planning. Recommendations should be shaped around each farm’s goals, resources, and operating conditions.

  • ✓Advice shaped around your farm goals, resources and operating conditions.
  • ✓Simple plans and records that help make day-to-day work more consistent.
  • ✓A balanced view of animal welfare, risk management and commercial realities.

How we work

Clear steps. Practical progress.

Good consultation should leave you with decisions and actions—not just a conversation.

01

Understand the goal

Discuss your livestock focus, current challenges, resources, budget and desired outcome.

02

Look at current routines, facilities, records and constraints before making recommendations.

03
